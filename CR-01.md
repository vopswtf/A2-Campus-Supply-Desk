# CR-01: Express Delivery

## Client request

Add express delivery alongside pickup and standard delivery.

## Acceptance requirements

1. Accept `express` for quotes and orders through the service API, individual commands, and interactive menu.
2. Express always costs **1,200 cents ($12.00 CAD)**. The fee is never waived, even when standard delivery would be free.
3. Student discount eligibility and rounding remain unchanged. The discount applies to merchandise, not delivery charges.
4. Preserve pickup and standard delivery behavior, including the existing free-standard-delivery threshold.
5. Quotes and placed orders must agree. Express orders retain their fulfillment, original items, delivery fee, discount, and total when retrieved, reopened from a save file, and cancelled.
6. Preserve existing validation, stock, order numbering, history, and cancellation rules. Other fulfillment values remain invalid.
7. Update menu prompts, command help, and relevant documentation so express is discoverable. Keep regular customer and pickup defaults unchanged.

No addresses, timing guarantees, external services, or real payments are required.

## Examples

All amounts below are Canadian dollars:

- Regular customer, two notebooks, express: $10 merchandise + $12 delivery = **$22**.
- Student customer, two hoodies, express: $80 merchandise - $8 discount + $12 delivery = **$84**.
- Regular customer, three hoodies: standard delivery total **$120**; express total **$132**.

```sh
python -m supplydesk quote --customer student --fulfillment express --item hoodie:2
```

Expected `total_cents`: `8400`.
